This policy explains what information Send An Alarm ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Send An Alarm lets family members set alarms that ring on each other's phones, but only after each person agrees.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show ads. By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. How we use your information

  • To run the app: create your account, set up your family, deliver alarms to the right phone, and tell the sender when an alarm was heard.
  • To enforce consent: an alarm reaches someone only after they agree to receive alarms from that person. You can allow or block people at any time.
  • To send notifications: invites, alarm requests, and alarm status updates.
  • To manage subscriptions: check whether your family has Family Plus and unlock its features.
  • To improve the app: understand which features are used, find crashes, and fix bugs.
  • To keep the app safe: prevent abuse and fraud, and meet legal obligations.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep your account, family and alarm data while your account is active. Alarms, including one-time alarms that have rung, are kept until you delete them or delete your account, so senders can see whether they were heard.

Analytics and crash data sent to PostHog is kept for up to 1 year, the retention period of PostHog's free plan, and is then deleted by PostHog.

When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days. We may keep limited records, such as processing logs, for up to 1 year where the law requires it, for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, or for security and fraud prevention. Analytics events already sent to PostHog are removed on our request or expire within the 1-year period. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

6. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: use the Delete account option in the app and confirm. This removes you from your family, cancels alarms you set, and deletes your data. It cannot be undone.
  • Without the app: email pixelartgames000@gmail.com from the address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my account". You can use the same address to ask us to delete only some of your data, such as your alarms, without deleting your account. We will complete your request and confirm within 30 days.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

A family member can remove you from their family. Your alarms are then removed from their phone, and you can no longer set alarms for them.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure with access controls. Sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or limit some processing, and to withdraw consent. This includes rights under the GDPR (EU/UK), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and US state laws such as the CCPA. To use these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Children

Send An Alarm is meant for families, but accounts are for people aged 13 and older (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from younger children. Users under 18 may use the app only with the consent of a parent or guardian. Where the law requires verifiable parental consent, such as under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, a parent or guardian must give that consent before a child's account is created. If you believe a child has given us data without this consent, contact us and we will delete it.
